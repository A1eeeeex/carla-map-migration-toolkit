# Golden Map rights checklist

Complete before publishing any evidence or media. Permission to use a map locally
does not imply permission to redistribute its exports, textures or screenshots.

- [ ] Identify the owner and record explicit rights for each source/derived asset.
- [ ] List third-party dependencies and their redistribution conditions.
- [ ] Obtain separate permission for public screenshots/video and derived evidence.
- [ ] Keep customer names, raw paths, usernames, IPs and raw logs out of public files.
- [ ] Use a stable anonymous public map alias; keep the private mapping local.
- [ ] Review every public file and caption, not just the enclosing archive.
- [ ] Retain permission evidence privately; publish a safe summary and attribution.
- [ ] Keep manifest rights status REVIEW_REQUIRED until the actual rights review is complete.

The repository's Apache-2.0 license does not relicense RoadRunner, CARLA, Unreal
or third-party content. Map/engine binaries remain outside Git even for a
rights-cleared case. This pass does not authorize asset uploads or relaxing
[publication rules](../../../../AGENTS.md). A later public media contribution
needs its exact allowlist entries and a reviewed policy update before commit.
